Hobbies

Are you ready for Ramblemania 5? Well... Even if you aren't, here we go! I am not sure how to format this section of the website and I was debating on splitting this into two sections. If you have read my CV then you already have a good idea of the things that I may like to do during my spare time. Growing up, I always wanted to learn how to play an instrument, and, unfortunately, neither I nor my family could afford to buy instruments. I learned how to play the piano during my teenage years but I have since forgotten how to play it. When I was young I also wanted to be a basketball player. I was a big fan of Michael Jordan. Unfortunately, there were no opportunities for me to pursue this dream. Clubs for people with disabilities did not exist at the time, unless you were papaplegic. I'm not sure what things look like today. It's been decades since I followed basketball. I have always been interested in history. Especially ancient history. My interest started with Egyptian history and continued with Greek and Roman history. I'm also interested in the time period that spans between the two world wars(1914-1945); among many, many other things.

As you may have deduced, if you read my CV. I have some interest in programming. Growing up, I was always fascinated by computers and in particular video games. They are like books, movies, games and music; all combined into one, cohesive, work of art. So, I always wanted to learn programming in the context of video games. Unfortunately, I could never afford the hardware to learn games development so that was something I got the chance to learn only recently. Yet, finding a job as a junior games developer is nigh impossible, especially now that AI is coming for all our jobs. I have always had a big interest int mathematic so that has also worked in my favor when learning programming. That's not to say I'm necessarily good at it. Like with everything else, it's all about practice! Among many other things, I also enjoy cooking! I particularly like asian cuisine, but I'm always trying to learn how to cook new food.
